The problem

Fraud moves in seconds. Alerts arrive too late — or cry wolf.

Account takeover

New device + MFA change + cash-out transfer while the owner still thinks they’re logged in.

Delayed restaurant posts

Bills land days later. Without GPS context, every late charge looks suspicious.

Foreign merchant labels

Online orders often show overseas descriptors even when you’re at home.

Passive alerts

“Call your bank” doesn’t stop the next transfer while an attacker is mid-session.

How it works

Agent investigates. Policy decides. Bank enforces.

1 · IngestEvents / Demo Lab scenarios hit AWS API Gateway → Lambda
2 · InvestigateOpenAI tools: txns, GPS match, security events, devices
3 · PolicyDeterministic engine authorizes or denies freeze
4 · ActMock bank restrict + verify state in MongoDB
5 · ProveFollow-up transfer → rejected_restricted
6 · NotifyUrgent local alerts + consumer statuses
